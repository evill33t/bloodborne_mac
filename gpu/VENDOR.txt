Retained shadPS4 sources for the macOS Metal port

Vendored from https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4 commit
5aa7ffa3f4f21bba827d7865ed8f69f98f993542 (0.20.1 WIP, GPL-2.0-or-later),
merged onto the port's tree from b4e7ae776071ceb1dd35004577a8719481e1d258.

The retained gpu/shadps4 subset contains:
- common/types.h, used by the macOS THD network adapter;
- core/libraries/gnmdriver/gnmdriver_init.h, used by the Metal GNM implementation;
- selected video_core/host_shaders GLSL inputs and FSR 1 helper headers, compiled
  to SPIR-V and translated to MSL by the native development tools.

Original copyright and license notices remain in those files. The AMD FidelityFX
headers retain their embedded MIT notices. Local shader changes retain bbport
comments. The old Vulkan renderer, SPIR-V emitter, GPU shims and their unused
third-party libraries and submodules have been removed.

The production direct GCN-to-MSL compiler and Metal renderer have their own
source record at gpu/metal/vendor/bloodborne/UPSTREAM.md. The selected engine,
plugin and network integration is recorded in gpu/metal/vendor/bbhost/UPSTREAM.md
and gpu/bbnet/SHADNET-PORT.md.
